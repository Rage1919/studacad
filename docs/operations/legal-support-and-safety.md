# Legal, support, and safety operations

## Published policy set

Studacad publishes a versioned Privacy Notice, Terms of Use, Tutor Agreement, Community Guidelines, Safety and Safeguarding policy, Cancellation and Refund Policy, Cookie Information, and Accessibility Statement. The current version is `2026-08-20`; the policy register stores a SHA-256 fingerprint of each published page and a six-month review date.

Registration records Terms and Privacy acknowledgement after email verification. Booking records Terms, Cancellation and Refund, and Safety acceptance. Course purchase records Terms and Cancellation and Refund acceptance. Tutor submission records Tutor Agreement and Privacy acceptance. Acceptance records are append-only and tied to the policy version, context, and request or entity reference.

Policy text is an operational draft, not evidence of professional advice. Before public launch, an administrator must obtain qualified Botswana and relevant cross-border legal, privacy, safeguarding, and accessibility review. The administrator records only truthful completed reviews in `/admin/policy-review`; attestations are append-only. Review again at least every six months and whenever product practices, processors, jurisdictions, or applicable rules materially change.

## Support service

Authenticated users open private cases at `/contact`. Each receives a durable `STU-YYYYMM-NNNNNN` reference. Normal cases target an initial response within one business day; safety cases are urgent and target four hours. These targets are operating goals, not guaranteed resolution times.

The admin queue at `/admin/support` orders work by response deadline. Only an administrator may assign, prioritize, or resolve a case. Public replies notify the requester; internal notes remain admin-only. Messages and policy attestations are append-only, and mutations create audit events.

Support staff should:

1. Confirm the requester, scope, and immediate risk without asking for unnecessary personal data.
2. Escalate imminent danger to local emergency services; Studacad is not an emergency service.
3. Limit case access to staff who need it and never copy case content to unapproved channels.
4. Preserve evidence, block contact where appropriate, and link booking or tutor reports to the case.
5. Record the outcome, user-facing explanation, and any follow-up date before resolving.

## Privacy requests

Use the `privacy` category for access, correction, portability/export, objection, or deletion requests. Verify account control before disclosing or changing data. Export and deletion remain controlled admin workflows: honor the request unless a disclosed legal, safety, fraud-prevention, accounting, or dispute-retention obligation requires limited preservation. Record scope, identity check, exceptions, completion, and response date in the private case.

## Incident and safeguarding escalation

Tutor reports create a private linked case and are not exposed to the reported tutor. For credible safety reports, an administrator should promptly review the booking, contact blocks, relevant in-app message records, and tutor-verification evidence, suspend access when proportionate, and preserve the audit trail. Do not promise confidentiality that cannot be maintained for a lawful safety investigation.

## Cookie operation

The application currently uses necessary authentication, security, and preference storage only. It does not ship advertising or optional analytics cookies. If non-essential tracking is later introduced, update the cookie page, complete the appropriate review, and add consent controls before enabling it.
